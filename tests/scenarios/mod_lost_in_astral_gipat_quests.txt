# Lost in Astral's Gipat quests in gz11g and gz12g (MOD2a; docs/notes/mods.md, "Lost in Astral played (MOD2)"): the
# quest is given through its variable (as its briefing does), its steps are played by moving the hero to the places
# its scripts watch (SetCPFast) and switching the key levers of gz12g (4523 to 4525) by script, and the quest's end
# and the briefing it opens next are checked. The key levers do not open from the quick panel's key in this zone
# (the hero stops short of them; MOD2b). On the vanilla data only the menu runs.
mod starter\Mods\LostInAstral
reject Script error
waitmenu
digest off
rebirth console thingamabob
rebirth newgame
rebirth waitfor 20 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
rebirth waitline 20 Saved savea

rebirth zone gz11g 0
rebirth waitzone 25 gz11g
rebirth waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
rebirth console @GodMode(0,1)
rebirth console @GSSetVar(0,"q.gz11g.q11g",1)
rebirth waitfor 10 IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.gz11g.q11g.1"),1)
rebirth digest
rebirth console @SetCPFast(GetLeader(),364,137,0)
rebirth waitfor 10 IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.gz11g.q11g.2"),1)
rebirth console @SetCPFast(GetLeader(),315,217,0)
rebirth waitfor 10 IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.gz11g.q11g.3"),1)
rebirth console @SetCPFast(GetLeader(),245,260,0)
rebirth waitfor 10 IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.gz11g.q11g"),2)
rebirth check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"b.bz14g.brief_118"),1)
rebirth digest

rebirth zone gz12g 0
rebirth waitzone 25 gz12g
rebirth waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
rebirth waitwindow 15 battle
rebirth console @GodMode(0,1)
rebirth console @GiveQuestItem(0,"PalantirBroken00")
rebirth console @GSSetVar(0,"q.gz12g.q27g",1)
rebirth waitfor 10 IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.gz12g.q27g.1"),1)
rebirth digest
rebirth console @SetCPFast(GetLeader(),110,119,0)
rebirth waitfor 10 IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.gz12g.q27g.2"),1)
rebirth console @SwitchLeverState(GetObject(4523),1)
rebirth waitfor 20 IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.gz12g.q27g.2"),2)
rebirth console @SwitchLeverState(GetObject(4524),1)
rebirth waitfor 20 IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.gz12g.q27g.3"),2)
rebirth console @SwitchLeverState(GetObject(4525),1)
rebirth waitfor 20 IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.gz12g.q27g"),2)
rebirth check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"b.Witch.brief_148"),1)
rebirth digest
report
quit
