# R17b: the ambient occlusion in a cave (docs/notes/renderer.md section 13, item 11): the Dragon's Cave gz5g
# (zone5_1, "#sky cave": its light fixed, mostly ambient), where the occlusion shows most. Frozen at the party's
# deploy point and with the camera turned: each frame with the occlusion, without it ("occlusion off") and the
# occlusion alone ("occlusion view"). msaa 8 and anisotropy 16 (the user's). Frames only;
# EI_RESOLUTION=3840x2160 gives the user's view.
render checkall
msaa 8
anisotropy 16
timeout 60
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 15 battle

zone gz5g 0
waitzone 25 gz5g
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @GodMode(0,1)
wait 2000
move 400 20
frames 5
freeze
capture cave
occlusion check
occlusion off
capture cave_off
occlusion view
capture cave_view
occlusion on
unfreeze
hold ctrl
keydown left
frames 40
keyup left
release ctrl
frames 5
freeze
capture cave_turned
occlusion off
capture cave_turned_off
occlusion view
capture cave_turned_view
occlusion on
unfreeze
report
quit
