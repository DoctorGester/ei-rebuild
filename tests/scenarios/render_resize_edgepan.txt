# R44: input in a pillarboxed window (docs/notes/renderer.md, "Resolution changes"). Full screen keeps the settings'
# 800x600 and scales the frame over the monitor without a display mode change, with black bars left and right on a
# wider monitor (on 16:9 the picture is the middle three quarters of the width). The mouse maps through the same
# transform as the picture: on the picture's first column (far from the window's edge; the harness settings' border
# scroll area is 1 pixel) the camera pans, over the right bar it pans too (the cursor put on the picture's edge), at
# the centre it does not; a click over the left bar is not passed on (logged).
render checkall
resolution 800x600
fullscreen 1
timeout 60
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 15 battle
move 400 300
waitpresent 10
frames 30

camtrace start
move 400 300
frames 40
camtrace stop still

# On the picture's first column: pans left.
camtrace start
move 0 300
frames 10
camtrace stop edge
move 400 300
frames 30

# Over the right bar (frame x 840): the cursor on the picture's right edge, pans right.
camtrace start
move 840 300
frames 10
camtrace stop bar
move 400 300
frames 30
camsteadier still bar pan 0.01
camsteadier still edge pan 0.01

# A click over the bar is ignored.
click -40 300
frames 3
expectlog input: a press outside the picture ignored
move 400 300
frames 10
report
quit
