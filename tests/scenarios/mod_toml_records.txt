# Records as TOML (docs/notes/mods.md, "Records as TOML"): a mod of our own, tools/make_mod_s19d.py toml, written
# into the run's harness_saves\s19d_mod, which "mod" makes the settings' Mod (either install). Its config.reg is
# config.reg.toml only; its Res\texts\texts.toml changes one string of the game's texts.res and its
# Res\database\acks.db.toml one record of the game's acks.db. At the main menu:
# - the interface's text "string quit" is the mod's (a string neither has stays none);
# - the acks database the units speak from has the mod's Haburu scenario list (s24's sound changed, s25 gone: a list
#   given replaces the game's whole list) and the game's records the mod leaves (HadExpW's s7);
# - records merge by field (MF3a): the mod gives only the mercenary warrior's attack list (his move list is the game's),
#   only the stone blood print's wet alpha (its dry look and the rest of its wet look are the game's) and only the
#   chance of the cow's animation "cidle01" (its frames, key, flags and moment are the game's).
# prepare: make_mod_s19d.py toml
mod harness_saves\s19d_mod
reject Script error
waitmenu
modtext quit MF3 mod text
modtext no_such_string_mf3 -
modack Haburu s24 Acks\Haburu\Scenario\MF3.wav
modack Haburu s25 -
modack HadExpW s7 Acks\HadExpW\Scenario\HadExpW.wav
modrecord ack Human Mercenary Warrior/2 = 1 Acks\HMW\Attack\MF3a.wav
modrecord ack Human Mercenary Warrior/1 = 6 Acks\HMW\Move\1.wav
modrecord blood stone = dry 0.45 900 150 wet 0.25 225 150
modrecord anim unanhoco/cidle01 = chance 37 frames 50 key 80590000 flags c moment 23
report
quit
