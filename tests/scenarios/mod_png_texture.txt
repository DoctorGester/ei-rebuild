# Textures as PNG (docs/notes/mods.md, "Textures as PNG"): a mod of our own, tools/make_mod_s19d.py png, written into
# the run's harness_saves\s19d_mod, which "mod" makes the settings' Mod (either install). Its Res\textures\ holds zone
# 1's terrain pages Zone1000 .. Zone1007 as PNGs of four coloured quadrants (render_loose_bmp's pictures), without
# the settings chunk: each is made into the game's own .mmp of that name (its format and level count, the levels by
# the box filter) and stands for it. The ground around the hero shows the quadrants (hues), and the trace has the
# PNG read for the .mmp. The PNG is the right way up (render_loose_bmp's .bmp comes out upside down), so the view
# holds the colours in other shares (green 7.4%).
# prepare: make_mod_s19d.py png
mod harness_saves\s19d_mod
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob

newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 10 battle
move 400 60
wait 1000
freeze
capture world
unfreeze
hues world 0.05 0.25
read mod Res\textures\Zone1000.mmp yes
report
quit
