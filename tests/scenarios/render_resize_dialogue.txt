# R46: a base's dialogue drawn again after a resolution change (docs/notes/renderer.md, "Resolution changes"): the
# base screen of bz1g (the briefing window) windowed at 1280x720, Smith's topics resized to 1000x800, a topic's
# phrases (the text panel and the two item panels) resized back to 1280x720. The briefing places everything in the
# 4:3 part, so nothing is built again. Smith's topic "M4" opened with its script variable, as ui_gallery_base.
# Captures to look at: topics_wide, topics_narrow, phrase_narrow, phrase_wide.
render checkall
resolution 1280x720
fullscreen 0
timeout 60
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
waitfor 40 IsUnitBlocked(GetLeader())
zone bz1g 0
waitzone 20 bz1g
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
check IsAlive(GetObjectByName("Smith"))
console @GSSetVar(0,"b.Smith.M4",1)
console @MoveToObject(GetLeader(),GetObjectByName("Smith"))
waitfor 30 IsLess(DistanceUnitUnit(GetLeader(),GetObjectByName("Smith")),4)
waitunit 10 Smith
clickunit Smith
frames 10
freeze
capture topics_wide
unfreeze
windowsize 1000 800
waitpresent 10
frames 5
expectlog Resolution: 1000x800 (was 1280x720)
freeze
capture topics_narrow
unfreeze
# The topic (region 0): the people walk to their places, then the first phrase.
clickregion 0
wait 4000
freeze
capture phrase_narrow
unfreeze
windowsize 1280 720
waitpresent 10
frames 5
expectlog Resolution: 1280x720 (was 1000x800)
freeze
capture phrase_wide
unfreeze
key esc
wait 800
key esc
wait 800
check IsAlive(GetLeader())
report
quit
