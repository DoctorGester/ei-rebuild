# A mod's zone (docs/notes/mods.md, "Zones from a mod"): the showcase mod's Res\texts\map.toml adds the zone
# "showcase" (its map folder Maps\showcase\ with no .mpr, its objects Maps\showcase.mob.toml with no .mob, its AI graph
# built at the load) and merges an exit 3 into the game's gz1g. Checked:
# - a new game on the first island (the game's zone1): the hero walks onto exit 3's area (63, 134)-(69, 142), east of
#   the landing's ruins, and is ordered to another point of it (a click there, off the hero): the leave question, and the party arrives on the
#   island in the island's exit 1's deploy rectangle (49, 133)-(59, 143): the island's script runs (its console line,
#   the quest's variable 1), the golem stands;
# - back: the hero walks onto the island's exit 1 area (42, 134)-(47, 142) and is ordered there; it leads to gz1g's
#   exit 3: the party arrives in gz1g in that exit's deploy rectangle (57, 135)-(61, 141), the zone loaded from its save;
# - and in again: the island loaded from its save (its graph built again: a save holds none for it), the golem and the
#   quest's variable as they were.
# prepare: make_showcase_mod.py
mod harness_saves\showcase_mod

newgame

console @GodMode(0,1)

waitfor 30 IsGreater(GetX(GetLeader()),64)

waitwindow 30 msgbox
key enter
waitzone 30 showcase
waitline 20 Showcase mod: the island loads

check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.showcase.showcase_golem"),1)
check IsGreater(GetX(GetLeader()),48)

check IsLess(GetY(GetLeader()),144)

waitwindow 10 battle

check IsLess(GetX(GetLeader()),62)
