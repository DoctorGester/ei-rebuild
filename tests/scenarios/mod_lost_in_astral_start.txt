# Lost in Astral's opening played through (MOD2; docs/notes/mods.md, "Lost in Astral played (MOD2)"): the new game in
# gz1g (the mod's zonezero), its first script areas, the walk into the camp bz1r, the recruiter's briefing (the
# mod's texts and speech) clicked through, the way back to gz1g by its exit 2 with the quest q1r, the quest's areas,
# the chest lever that gives the quest item TeleportKey00, the portal lever, which takes that key from the quick
# item panel (its lock is by id; a plain click does not open it), and the portal: QuestComplete, the movie
# teleprt1.bik from the mod's Movies\ and the zone change to the next allod's base bz1h.
# The walks: the hero walks to the first area and to the camp; the quest areas, the levers' ground and the portal's
# platform are reached with SetCPFast (on the way a flying creature attacks and holds the hero in a fight; GodMode).
# On the vanilla data (no starter\Mods) only the menu runs: every later step is Rebirth's.
# Only on Rebirth's data: the vanilla run checks nothing another scenario does not.
# installs: rebirth
mod starter\Mods\LostInAstral
reject Script error
waitmenu
rebirth console thingamabob
rebirth newgame
rebirth waitfor 20 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
rebirth waitline 20 Saved savea
rebirth console @GodMode(0,1)
rebirth waitwindow 15 battle
rebirth check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"b.First.brief_0"),0)

# The first area (23, 121): object 1664521 attacks; the camp's area (54, 146) leaves to bz1r.
rebirth console @MoveToPoint(GetLeader(),23,121)
rebirth waitfor 30 IsLess(DistanceUnitPoint(GetLeader(),23,121),3)
rebirth console @MoveToPoint(GetLeader(),54,146)
rebirth waitzone 40 bz1r
rebirth waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)

# The camp: the hero walks to the recruiter ("First"), the briefing brief_0 opens by itself.
rebirth waitwindow 20 briefing
rebirth waitdialogue 20 3
rebirth talk 40
rebirth check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"b.First.brief_0"),2)
rebirth waitzone 40 gz1g
rebirth waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
rebirth waitwindow 15 battle
rebirth console @GodMode(0,1)
rebirth wait 3000
rebirth check IsLess(DistanceUnitPoint(GetLeader(),50.5,149.5),1)
rebirth check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.gz1g.q1r"),1)

# The quest's areas 2 (100, 174) and 3 (100, 90).
rebirth console @SetCPFast(GetLeader(),100,174,0)
rebirth wait 2000
rebirth check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.gz1g.q1r.1"),2)
rebirth console @SetCPFast(GetLeader(),100,90,0)
rebirth wait 2000
rebirth check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.gz1g.q1r.2"),2)

# The chest lever 3340943: a click; the script gives TeleportKey00.
rebirth waitunit 10 #3340943
rebirth clickunit #3340943
rebirth waitfor 20 IsEqual(GetLeverState(GetObject(3340943)),1)
rebirth wait 1000
rebirth check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.gz1g.q1r.4"),1)

# The portal lever 43974: the key from the quick item panel (its top row), then the lever.
rebirth console @SetCPFast(GetLeader(),43,62,0)

rebirth camera 45 62

rebirth frames 10
rebirth click 725 525
rebirth frames 5

rebirth frames 5
rebirth clickunit #43974
rebirth waitfor 20 IsEqual(GetLeverState(GetObject(43974)),1)
rebirth wait 5000
rebirth check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.gz1g.q1r.5"),1)

# The portal: area 4 (53.5, 50.5) on its platform.
rebirth console @SetCPFast(GetLeader(),53.5,50.5,0)
rebirth waitzone 40 bz1h
rebirth waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
rebirth check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.gz1g.q1r"),2)
rebirth check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"b.bz1h.brief_2"),1)
