# Magic traps with someone inside (src/game/objects/traps.cpp: MagicTrap::RunTick, UpdateVictims, StaysInside,
# FireAtOccupants, CastAtUnits; docs/notes/units.md, "Magic traps"). magic_traps.txt and magic_traps_victims.txt put
# a wolf in a circle, but the trap's side mask is 1 (side 0, the hero's) and the wolf walks off to the hero before the
# first tick, so no trap ever found a victim. Here tools/make_trap_mob.py moves the first circle of zone2's first
# lightning trap (MTF1) to the hero's start (54, 138, radius 5) and writes it twice: 30001 with interval 0 (asks for
# newcomers each tick: the units inside are kept, and dropped when they leave or die) and 30011 with interval 3
# (fires at whoever stands inside every third tick). The hero is in god mode and is the first victim; a wolf of the
# hero's player is the second (a lightning bolt of element 2 kills a tutor wolf: StaysInside drops the dead). The hero
# then walks out of the circle and back (dropped and a newcomer again); a disarmed trap (ActivateTrap 0) stays quiet.
# No `speed`, so a golden can be recorded.
# prepare: make_trap_mob.py --interval 0 --count 1 --circle 54 138 5
# prepare: make_trap_mob.py --interval 3 --count 1 --circle 54 138 5 --first-id 30011 --out traps2.mob
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
console filter all on
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
waitfor 15 IsUnitBlocked(GetLeader())
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
console @GodMode(0,1)
console @AddMob("..\saves\traps.mob")
console @AddMob("..\saves\traps2.mob")
waitfor 10 Mul(IsEqual(GetObjectID(GetObject(30001)),30001),IsEqual(GetObjectID(GetObject(30011)),30011))
wait 1500
check IsAlive(GetLeader())

# A wolf of the hero's side steps into the circles and is struck down.
console @AddUnitToServer("TrapWolf","WolfTutor","TrapWolf",Add(GetX(GetLeader()),1),GetY(GetLeader()),0)
waitexpect 10 damaged to death
check Not(IsAlive(GetObjectByName("TrapWolf")))

# The hero walks out of the circles and back in.
console @MoveToPoint(GetLeader(),Add(GetX(GetLeader()),12),GetY(GetLeader()))
waitfor 30 IsGreater(DistanceUnitPoint(GetLeader(),54,138),8)
wait 1000
console @MoveToPoint(GetLeader(),54,138)
waitfor 30 IsLess(DistanceUnitPoint(GetLeader(),54,138),3)
wait 1000

# Disarmed, nothing happens to a new wolf; armed again it is struck.
console @ActivateTrap(GetObject(30001),0)
console @ActivateTrap(GetObject(30011),0)
console @AddUnitToServer("TrapWolf2","WolfTutor","TrapWolf2",Add(GetX(GetLeader()),1),GetY(GetLeader()),0)
wait 2000
check IsAlive(GetObjectByName("TrapWolf2"))
console @ActivateTrap(GetObject(30011),1)
waitexpect 10 damaged to death
check Not(IsAlive(GetObjectByName("TrapWolf2")))
check IsAlive(GetLeader())
report
quit
