# R59a: what the snow and the rain cost a frame (docs/notes/renderer.md section 12, "Weather"). The snow map gz11k
# with the camera held: "renderstats" logs the weather's own build time and vertices (its draws' hand-over left out)
# and the main thread's CPU and wall time per frame (no vsync wait) in clear weather, in snow, in snow with its
# particles built but not drawn (weatherparticles off) and in rain at 1920x1080, and clear and snow at 3840x2160.
# Numbers only (no check): compare a run before and after a change, on a quiet machine. The server's weather comes and
# goes by itself (renderer.md, "Weather"), so each measure follows its own request closely.
