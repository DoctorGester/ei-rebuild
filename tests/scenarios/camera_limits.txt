# P12: the camera's limits, the catalog's defaults (settings_catalog.inc; the harness snapshot leaves them out): the
# pitch keys tilt from straight down (90) to 45 degrees up (-45), the zoom keys and the wheel reach the least distance
# 3 (CameraMinDistanceToCarrier and its hard limit; at 3 the near plane clears the hero at any tilt) and go back out,
# the up key pans forward whatever the pitch (Camera::Pan along the ground: the camera's own Y axis points back on
# the ground below level), and the camera stays high enough that no part of its near plane reaches under the ground
# (Camera::ClampAboveObjects with the near plane's reach; camrange ground: the plane's lowest height above the
# ground, 0 or more), at the slopes where a camera kept only by its point (CameraMinHeight 0) cut 0.3 to 0.6 into
# the ground, standing and while panning. Frames for the eye (build/captures/p12): straight down from 50, up at
# 45 degrees, two slopes looking up, and the least distance on the hero looking up.
timeout 60
render checkall
resolution 1920x1080
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 15 battle
climate 0
console @FixWorldTime(12)
move 400 300
frames 5

# The pitch keys: Ctrl + up to straight down, Ctrl + down to 45 degrees up.
hold ctrl
keydown up
frames 200
keyup up
release ctrl
frames 10
camrange pitch 89.9 90.1
hold ctrl
keydown down
frames 300
keyup down
release ctrl
frames 10
camrange pitch -45.1 -44.9
camrange ground 0 1000

# The zoom keys to the least distance and out; the wheel the same (100 notches in, 10 out: two steps a notch, 3 x 1.05^20).
keydown pgup
frames 200
keyup pgup
frames 10
camrange distance 3 3
camrange ground 0 1000
keydown pgdn
frames 100
keyup pgdn
frames 10
camrange distance 5 70
wheel 400 300 12000
frames 5
camrange distance 3 3
wheel 400 300 -1200
frames 5
camrange distance 7.9 8

# The up key pans forward whatever the pitch: heading 0 faces +y.
