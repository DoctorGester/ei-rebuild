# R46: the main menu's windows drawn again after a resolution change (docs/notes/renderer.md, "Resolution changes"):
# windowed at 1280x720, the options over the main menu's greyed copy resized to 1000x800 while open, then closed and
# opened again at that size; then their help page (a help window over the options' ungreyed copy: the backdrop of
# mode 4 over that of mode 2) resized back to 1280x720 and opened again. The frame after each resize is the frame of
# the windows opened at that size (samecapture; the game's clocks held from the resize to the second capture). The
# main menu itself at 1280x720 and 1000x800. Captures to look at: menu_wide, menu_narrow, options_narrow,
# help_wide (and the _fresh twins).
render checkall
resolution 1280x720
fullscreen 0
timeout 60
reject Script error
waitmenu
move 400 300
frames 5
freeze
capture menu_wide
unfreeze

# The options (region 3) over the main menu's greyed copy.
clickregion 3
waitwindow 10 options
frames 5
freeze
windowsize 1000 800
waitpresent 10
frames 5
expectlog Resolution: 1000x800 (was 1280x720)
capture options_narrow
key esc
waitwindow 10 menu
frames 5
clickregion 3
waitwindow 10 options
frames 5
capture options_narrow_fresh
unfreeze
samecapture options_narrow options_narrow_fresh

# Their help page (H) over the options' copy, resized back to 1280x720.
key h
waitwindow 10 help
frames 10
freeze
windowsize 1280 720
waitpresent 10
frames 5
expectlog Resolution: 1280x720 (was 1000x800)
capture help_wide
key esc
waitwindow 10 options
frames 5
key h
waitwindow 10 help
frames 10
capture help_wide_fresh
unfreeze
samecapture help_wide help_wide_fresh
key esc
waitwindow 10 options
key esc
waitwindow 10 menu

# The main menu at 1000x800.
windowsize 1000 800
waitpresent 10
frames 5
freeze
capture menu_narrow
unfreeze
report
quit
