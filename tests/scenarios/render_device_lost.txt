# R9c1: a lost Direct3D 11 device in the switch-over (docs/notes/renderer.md, section 9). "losedevice"
# makes the new renderer act as if the swap chain's Present had returned DXGI_ERROR_DEVICE_REMOVED
# (a real removal needs a driver reset or tools that are not installed): it releases the device,
# sokol and everything made on them, makes a new device, and the next frames make the frame target,
# the game window's swap chain, the window surfaces (from the game's memory) and the .mmp textures
# (from the file levels kept for them) again. At one frozen moment the frame before the loss and the
# frame after it must match, in the main menu (window surfaces, the cursor) and in the world
# (terrain, figures, objects, the interface's textures); then the game goes on to the camp, whose
# textures are loaded on the new device, and the device is lost once more there.
render new,present
reject Script error
waitmenu
waitpresent 10
console thingamabob

# The main menu.
move 400 20
wait 500
freeze
capture menu
losedevice 10
waitpresent 10
capture menu_again
unfreeze
samecapture menu menu_again

# The hero on zone1, unblocked and standing still, the camera settled on him.
newgame
speed 10
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 10 battle
move 400 60
wait 1000
freeze
capture world
losedevice 10
waitpresent 10
capture world_again
unfreeze
samecapture world world_again

# The camp (screen 5), loaded on the new device, and a loss there; Esc returns to the game.
screen 5
waitwindow 15 camp
move 400 20
wait 300
freeze
capture camp
losedevice 10
waitpresent 10
capture camp_again
unfreeze
samecapture camp camp_again
key esc
waitwindow 15 battle
waitpresent 10
report
quit
