# R9c4: the options window's settings applied in the world while our renderer presents
# (docs/notes/renderer.md, section 9, R9c4). The game has no resolution in its options (the 26
# settings at 00787430; VideoResolution is read from the registry once, and the renderer and its
# device are made once by WinMain), so there is no device re-creation to exercise. The settings the
# OK applies that reach the renderer are the shadow switches (7-9: units, buildings, flora): OK
# (Apply 006524A0) drops the shadows of every client object whose kind has them off (DropShadows
# 0056D7A0), and switched on again they are rasterised anew (the shadow textures made again on the
# CPU and uploaded, `TextureChanged` / `HolderGone`).
#
# Group 0 (region 2) holds the three as its rows 0-2 (spin controls; Enter steps the selected one,
# Down selects the next). The world: the hero on zone1, blocked and standing; captures at frozen
# moments with the shadows as they started, off (or on, when they started off), and back.
render new,present
reject Script error
waitmenu
frames 5
console thingamabob

newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 10 battle
move 400 60
wait 1000
freeze
capture shadows
unfreeze
