# What the image's renderer does per frame (R16; docs/notes/renderer.md section 13): "renderstats"
# logs, and puts into the report, the averages since the last one (hand-over, GPU uploads, draws and
# state changes, images made, CPU time) for scenes the renderer plan is measured on: the main menu,
# zone1 outdoors, a fight of many units there, the base bz1g (a town), and the interface screens
# (camp, global map, escape menu) with the mouse moving over them. zone1, the fight and the town are
# measured again with the classic shadows (R16j: the game's decals instead of the shadow map), zone1
# with its terrain pages built and uploaded every frame (R16c: as before the pages were kept on the GPU),
# zone1, the fight and the town with their figure meshes built and uploaded every frame (R16d: as before
# the meshes were kept on the GPU), the classic fight and town and the global map with the textures in the
# renderer's memory made again as new images (R16b: as before they were updated in place). Counts only, but no terrain page or mesh kept on the GPU may have been
# found stale.
# no original: vanilla rebirth (the counts are our renderer's)
render new,present
timeout 120
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
frames 20
renderstats -
move 300 200
frames 20
move 500 400
frames 20
renderstats menu

newgame

waitfor 15 IsUnitBlocked(GetLeader())
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 15 battle
waitpresent 10

frames 10
renderstats -
frames 60
renderstats zone1
shadows classic
frames 60
renderstats zone1_classic
shadows map
terraincache off
frames 60
renderstats zone1_fresh
terraincache on
meshcache off
frames 60
renderstats zone1_meshfresh
meshcache on
