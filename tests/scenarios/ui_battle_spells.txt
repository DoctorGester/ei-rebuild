# The battle screen's spell target modes (docs/notes/ui.md, "Seventeenth pass"). The hero gets
# three spells (GiveUnitSpell) and a click on its portrait refreshes the spell column (00637DC0 ->
# 0063E820): the spells' buttons stand at x 760..800, the last one at y 470..510 and each earlier
# one 40 higher (0063E4B0), so with three spells their centres are (780, 410), (780, 450) and
# (780, 490). A click on a button (CHudSpellColumn::Click 0063EC90) starts its target mode
# (+0x17C: 1 for a spell with data flag 0x10000000, else 2) and "clickunit" on a wolf ends it
# through SelectUnit (00638EF0): mode 1 is the spell on a unit (00637500), mode 2 the spell at the
# unit's position (00637750). The wolves stand blocked next to the blocked hero; the report's call
# counts for CBattleScreenSetup::UseOnUnit / UseAtPoint show which were reached.
# Coordinates are the 800x600 layout. The game runs ~5.5x faster (10 ms per world update).
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
speed 10
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),1)
console @GodMode(0,1)
console @GSSetVar(0,"hx",GetX(GetLeader()))
console @GSSetVar(0,"hy",GetY(GetLeader()))
console @GiveUnitSpell(GetLeader(),"lightning")
console @GiveUnitSpell(GetLeader(),"firewall")
console @GiveUnitSpell(GetLeader(),"fireball")
waitwindow 15 battle

# The hero's portrait: selects it and shows its spells.
click 400 560
frames 3
check IsAlive(GetLeader())

# The action column (0..40 x 270..510; button i at y 470 - 40 i .. 510 - 40 i): with the hero
# selected, button 0 is "skill0011" (action -1: follow, mode 7) and button 1 the science button
# (action 0: mode 8). CHudActionColumn::Select (0063FB50): button 0, then button 1 (moves the
# highlight), then button 1 again (drops the pending action, so the spells below start clean).
click 20 490
wait 200
click 20 450
wait 200
click 20 450
wait 200

console @AddUnitToServer("SpellWolf1","WolfTutor","SpellWolf1",Add(GSGetVar(0,"hx"),3),GSGetVar(0,"hy"),0)
console @AddUnitToServer("SpellWolf2","WolfTutor","SpellWolf2",GSGetVar(0,"hx"),Add(GSGetVar(0,"hy"),3),0)
console @AddUnitToServer("SpellWolf3","WolfTutor","SpellWolf3",Sub(GSGetVar(0,"hx"),4),GSGetVar(0,"hy"),0)
waitfor 10 IsAlive(GetObjectByName("SpellWolf1"))
waitfor 10 IsAlive(GetObjectByName("SpellWolf2"))
waitfor 10 IsAlive(GetObjectByName("SpellWolf3"))
console @BlockUnit(GetObjectByName("SpellWolf1"),1)
console @BlockUnit(GetObjectByName("SpellWolf2"),1)
console @BlockUnit(GetObjectByName("SpellWolf3"),1)
waitunit 10 SpellWolf1
waitunit 10 SpellWolf2
waitunit 10 SpellWolf3

# Each spell's button, then a wolf.
click 780 410
wait 200
clickunit SpellWolf1
wait 500
click 780 450
wait 200
clickunit SpellWolf2
wait 500
click 780 490
wait 200
clickunit SpellWolf3
wait 1000
check IsAlive(GetLeader())
report
quit
