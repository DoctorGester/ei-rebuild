# Shadows of objects outside the view (R20; docs/notes/renderer.md section 13 item 10): the world's draw
# hands the shadow map the objects the game does not draw whose bounds, seen from the sun, are in the
# map's area and in front of the visible ground's far side (src/game/world_client_shadow_casters.cpp).
# At frozen moments the frame with them ("shadowcasters all", the default) is captured, and:
# - with every caster taken from that walk and none from the game's draws ("walked"): the frame
#   matches, so the walk places, cuts out and selects the meshes as the game's draws do;
# - without them ("view": only what the game draws casts, as before R20): at zone1's start and in the
#   town bz1g nothing outside the view reaches the visible ground (the sun is behind the scene, the
#   shadows fall towards the camera); with the town's camera moved towards the viewer until the gate
#   leaves the top of the screen, its long shadow stays on the road with them and is gone without them
#   (the shadow that popped in at the screen's edge, the user's report).
# shadowcheck requires casters outside the view and every object the game shadows in the map.
# no original: vanilla rebirth (the shadow map is our renderer's)
render checkall
timeout 120
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
waitfor 15 IsUnitBlocked(GetLeader())
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 15 battle
waitpresent 10
move 400 20
frames 5
freeze
capture start_all
shadowcheck 1 1
renderstats -
shadowcasters view
capture start_view
renderstats start_view
shadowcasters all
capture start_all2
renderstats start_all
shadowcasters walked
capture start_walked
shadowcasters all
unfreeze
samecapture start_all start_all2
samecapture start_all start_walked 8 0.01
samecapture start_all start_view 8 0.01

renderstats town_view

capture town_all2
renderstats town_all
shadowcasters walked
capture town_walked

unfreeze
samecapture town_all town_all2

# The camera moved towards the viewer (Down held, 90 frames of 8 ms) until the gate is above the screen.
frametime 8
keydown down
frames 90
keyup down
frames 60
freeze
capture gate_all
shadowcheck 4 10
renderstats -
shadowcasters view
capture gate_view

shadowcasters all
capture gate_all2
renderstats gate_all
shadowcasters walked
capture gate_walked
shadowcasters all

samecapture gate_all gate_all2
samecapture gate_all gate_walked 8 0.01
# (R16i: the gate's shadow takes the sun's light only, not the town's fires': lighter where they reach.)
diffcapture gate_all gate_view 0.003 0.05
# The walk's casters are kept on the GPU by part (R37c): checked against fresh builds as the drawn meshes.
meshcache check
report
