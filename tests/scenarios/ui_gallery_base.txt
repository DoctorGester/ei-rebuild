# Interface gallery, a base's dialogue (ticket N4; docs/notes/ui-native.md, "N4: the gallery"): the
# steps of ui_base_topic, held still (freeze) and captured. Frames: base (the base screen of bz1g,
# the briefing window), base_topics (Smith's topics and "goodbye" after a click on him), base_phrase
# (the dialogue's phrase text), base_rewards (after the phrases: the rewards). Smith's topic "M4"
# is opened with its script variable (FindTopics reads "b.<person>.<topic>" = 1), as in ui_base_topic.
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
waitfor 40 IsUnitBlocked(GetLeader())
zone bz1g 0
waitzone 20 bz1g
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
check IsAlive(GetObjectByName("Smith"))
console @GSSetVar(0,"b.Smith.M4",1)
console @MoveToObject(GetLeader(),GetObjectByName("Smith"))
waitfor 30 IsLess(DistanceUnitUnit(GetLeader(),GetObjectByName("Smith")),4)
waitunit 10 Smith
freeze
capture base
unfreeze
clickunit Smith
frames 10
freeze
capture base_topics
unfreeze
# The topic (region 0): the people walk to their places, then the phrases (a click each).
clickregion 0
wait 4000
freeze
capture base_phrase
unfreeze
click 400 300
wait 800
click 400 300
wait 800
click 400 300
wait 800
# Esc: the rest of the phrases, then the rewards; Esc again finishes the topic.
key esc
wait 800
freeze
capture base_rewards
unfreeze
key esc
wait 800
check IsAlive(GetLeader())
report
quit
