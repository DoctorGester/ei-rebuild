# (the units larger in the frame) with msaa 8 and anisotropy 16 as the user plays: the hero with a wolf, a goblin, a
# boar and a skeleton beside him (blocked, skinned as the maps place them) on zone1, dry and after a minute of rain (wet,
# a sheen on the heads, shoulders and backs); on the snow map gz11k before and after two minutes of snow (snow on the
# upward parts). Four wheel turns of five notches zoom the camera in. Frames only (goldens hold none).
