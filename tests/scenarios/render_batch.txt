# Terrain seams (R12, docs/notes/renderer.md section 11): the ground pages sampled with point mips
# (D3DTFP_POINT, what the game asks for) jumped a texture level along the tiles' edges, which showed
# as seams moving with the camera. The image samples them anisotropically with linear mips. At two
# frozen views of zone1 (zoomed out, then moved by the screen edge) the image captures its frame, the
# terrain's level map through its own sampler (terrainlevels on) and through the old one (point);
# levelsteps requires few level steps in the first and many in the second (so the check sees seams);
# Rebirth's wide view looks further over steeper slopes and has more of both.
# mmplevels (R12b): every .mmp texture's levels, decoded as drawn, must agree with their level 0
# (averaged to 4 x 4: at most 52 seen); the levels read through the game's overflowing offset formula
# (bright green strips over the trees here) lay 128 / 149 off.

# the frame with them kept on the GPU.

# ---- section render_blink
# The blink (R11d; docs/notes/units.md, renderer.md section 7): a unit with a face texture
# ("<model>mouth": the mouth of the briefing's speakers) shows the face's open or closed rectangle
# over a rectangle of its skin. The original blits it into the skin's DirectDraw texture down the
# mip chain (00533980); the image's renderer draws it over the skin in its shader
# (render::TexturePatch). On zone1 (the faces loaded as on entering a zone: `blink faces`), frozen:
# open, closed and open again through the game's own blink (`blink`, forced, every unit with a face).
# The two open frames match; the closed one differs from them only at the hero's mouth in the
# portrait (top left, about 160 pixels of the 3840 x 2160 frame; the original's run the same). R16d:
# the open frame with the figure meshes built and uploaded every frame matches the one kept on the GPU.
