# The showcase mod's Chain Spark (examples/showcase_mod; docs/notes/mods.md, "The showcase mod"; docs/modding.md,
# "Spells"): a scripted spell with data effects of its own, cast by the hero from the HUD.
# - The quest gives it: taking the quest puts a spell container of "spark" in the party's things (Maps\zone1.lua's
#   line); the camp's magic page draws it with the mod's picture (Res\textures\spell0101.png) and puts it on the new
#   game's hero as it is (the record's skill 0 needs no elemental skill; its 8 mana are within the hero's 50).
# - The golem and its two shards have their AI modes cleared (UMClear: they stand and do not come at the hero, whose
#   fight would end the chain). The hero walks beside them (about 6 from each), where it sees them, and casts the
#   spell at the golem with its key (3) and a click. Res\spells\chain_spark.lua sends the spark (a missile carrying
#   the data effect ChainSpark, Res\textures\chainspark.png's pictures) at the golem; each strike damages the unit
#   (lightning), shocks it (the spell's own active effect: slowed by its OnStats, crackling with the record's look
#   ChainSparkShock), starts ChainSparkHit on it, draws an arc to the nearest enemy not struck yet within 5 (shard
#   4002, then 4003) and sends the spark along it 10 ticks later (a timer). Its game variables hold each strike's
#   damage: 12, 7.2 (12 x 0.6), 4.32 (7.2 x 0.6).
# - The game is saved after the first strike, mid-chain (one strike in the variables), and plays on to the three
#   strikes. The save is loaded: the chain goes on from it (its data, timer, arc and missile in the save) to the same
#   strikes, both shards struck.
# - Captures: four frames from the cast on (flight_1 to flight_4: the spark in flight, the first strike and its arc);
#   ten frames from the save on (chain_1 to chain_10): the leaps and the strikes on the shards.
# prepare: make_showcase_mod.py
mod harness_saves\showcase_mod
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitline 20 Showcase mod: the party has the Chain Spark
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
waitwindow 10 battle
console @GodMode(0,1)
console @UMClear(GetObject(4001))
console @UMClear(GetObject(4002))
console @UMClear(GetObject(4003))

# The camp's magic page: the quest's spell container drawn with the mod's picture, put on the hero.
screen 5
waitwindow 15 camp
clickregion 5
wait 500
campputon 3008 1
frames 3
read mod Res\textures\spell0101.mmp yes
key esc
waitwindow 15 battle
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)

# The hero's portrait: selects it and shows its spells (healing, 0x0C, the Chain Spark).
click 400 560
frames 3
hudstate

console @MoveToPoint(GetLeader(),80,122)
waitidle 30
check IsAlive(GetObject(4002))
check IsAlive(GetObject(4003))
camera 82 117 0 16 55
frames 3
waitunit 10 #4001
key 3
frames 2
clickunit #4001
frames 1
capture flight 4

waitfor 15 IsGreater(GSGetVar(0,"chain_spark.strikes"),0)
key esc
waitwindow 10 escmenu
click 395 120
waitwindow 10 saveload
clickregion 0
waitline 15 Saved save1
check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"chain_spark.strikes"),1)
waitwindow 10 battle
capture chain 10
waitfor 10 IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"chain_spark.strikes"),3)
check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"chain_spark.strike1"),12)
check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"chain_spark.strike2"),7.2)
check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"chain_spark.strike3"),4.32)
check IsLess(HP(GetObject(4002)),20)
check IsLess(HP(GetObject(4003)),20)
read mod Res\textures\chainspark.mmp yes
read mod Res\effects\ChainSpark.effect.toml yes
read mod Res\effects\ChainSparkHit.effect.toml yes
read mod Res\effects\ChainSparkShock.effect.toml yes
read mod Res\spells\chain_spark.lua yes

wait 300
load save1
waitwindow 30 battle
waitfor 20 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
dump loaded_save1
check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"chain_spark.strike1"),12)
waitfor 10 IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"chain_spark.strikes"),3)
check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"chain_spark.strike2"),7.2)
check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"chain_spark.strike3"),4.32)
check IsLess(HP(GetObject(4002)),20)
check IsLess(HP(GetObject(4003)),20)
check IsAlive(GetLeader())
report
quit
