# ---- section camera_spin
# The drawn view while the camera spins (R35a; docs/notes/renderer.md section 14, "Spinning"), at a fixed frame
# time of 8 ms. Zoomed in and tilted low, a turn with Ctrl and an arrow key swings the camera over the ground and
# the houses behind it: the game lifts it over them and aims it back down at the focus (CCamera::ClampAboveObjects
# and ApplyView, as the original does), so it rises and falls by several units a turn and, at 120 frames a second,
# jumps in height at every world tick; the drawn view spreads those jumps (its largest step in height about a quarter
# of the camera's; what is left are the game's own changes within a frame). Zoomed out, a fast drag with
# the right button (mousedrag 77 a frame, 160 counts a 60 Hz frame: 0.43 rad a tick) turns the camera faster than the drawn view's old cut
# (0.4 rad or 8 units a tick, shown at once): the drawn view still turns without jumps, and along the arc: its
# distance from the focus (reach) changes only as the camera's own does (spread along the chord, it pulled in and out
# at every tick: 144.6 units of change against the camera's 6.0, R35c).
# 1480 frames: 207 s on llvmpipe with 4 cores, and cut at 360 s (step 31 of 51) on CI's runner twice (H60; CI
# multiplies this by EI_TIME_SCALE).
waitline 15 Saved savea
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 15 battle
move 400 300
frametime 8
frames 30

keydown pgup
frames 150
keyup pgup
frames 40
hold ctrl
keydown down
frames 200
keyup down
frames 40
camtrace start
keydown left
frames 400
keyup left
frames 40
camtrace stop low
# Back up to about 30 degrees (0.6 a frame) for the fast turn: at the least pitch (-45 since P12) the camera rides
# the ground, and the drawn view's reach, spread over the frames, changes less than the camera's jumpy own.
keydown up
frames 125
keyup up
release ctrl
frames 10

keydown pgdn
frames 200
keyup pgdn
frames 40
rdown 400 300
camtrace start
mousedrag 77 0
frames 300
mousedrag 0 0
frames 40
camtrace stop fast
rup 400 300

camsame low low.camera turn 0.03
camsmooth low turn
camsmooth low.camera turn jerky
camsteadier low low.camera height 0.35
camsmooth low.camera height jerky
camsame fast fast.camera turn 0.03
camsmooth fast turn
camsmooth fast.camera turn jerky
camsame fast fast.camera reach 0.03
frametime 0

# ---- section camera_smooth
# The drawn view (R18, R35a; docs/notes/renderer.md section 14): at a fixed frame time of 8 ms (a 120 Hz screen's
# frames, on the virtual clock) the battle camera pans (an arrow key held), turns (Ctrl and an arrow key), zooms
# (Page Down) and follows the walking hero (Home). camtrace records each frame's drawn view and the camera's own:
# the camera jumps at every world tick (jerky: each client tick moves it by 55 ms of motion at once, on top of
# the frame's 8 ms, as the original's does), the drawn view spreads each tick's step over the frames up to the
# next tick, so it moves without jumps (camsmooth) and covers the same ground (camsame).
# Back to the main menu for the next section.
frames 5
ifwindow battle key esc
waitwindow 10 escmenu
clickregion 3
frames 5
key enter
waitwindow 15 menu
newgame
waitline 15 Saved savea
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 15 battle
move 400 300
frametime 8
frames 30

# Pan: held for 110 frames (880 ms: 16 ticks), then 60 frames to come to rest.
camtrace start
keydown left
frames 110
keyup left
frames 60
camtrace stop pan

# Turn.
hold ctrl
camtrace start
keydown left
frames 110
keyup left
frames 60
camtrace stop turn
release ctrl

# Zoom.
camtrace start
keydown pgdn
frames 55
keyup pgdn
frames 60
camtrace stop zoom

# Follow: the camera tracks the hero (Home) while it walks six units east.

camtrace stop follow
waitfor 20 IsGreater(GetX(GetLeader()),Add(GSGetVar(0,"x0"),5))

camsame pan pan.camera pan 0.03
camsame turn turn.camera turn 0.03
camsame zoom zoom.camera zoom 0.03

camsmooth pan.camera pan jerky
camsmooth turn turn
camsmooth turn.camera turn jerky
camsmooth zoom zoom
camsmooth zoom.camera zoom jerky
camsmooth follow pan
frametime 0
check IsAlive(GetLeader())

# ---- section camera
# Camera (src/engine/scene/camera.cpp): the per-frame update (CCamera::Update, ApplyView, SampleTerrain)
# while the hero walks, arrow-key panning (Pan), the saved-view keys F9-F12 (RestoreView), the
# "normal camera" key N, the script camera commands, and a recorded camera path (PlayCamera:
# PlaybackStart / PlaybackStep). keyboard.ini: F9-F12 camera1-4, N camera_norm, arrows scroll
# (F5 / F8 are quicksave / quickload: not used).

key enter
