# The battle screen's party commands (docs/notes/ui.md, "Sixteenth pass"). A click on the hero's
# portrait goes through SelectUnit (00638EF0) to the member toggle (006379E0), which makes the
# hero speak (slot 32, 006366F0). Then three wolves stand blocked next to the blocked hero (it
# stays where the camera put it, so ground clicks do not move the view) and a grid of clicks
# around the screen centre lands on them: a click on a hostile unit is the attack order
# (00637190). Then the wolves die and the same grid lands on the bodies: a click on a dead unit
# is 006373A0. Clicks that hit the ground give move orders (00636F10) the blocked hero ignores.
# The report's call counts for CBattleScreenSetup::AttackUnit / OrderDeadUnit show whether a
# click hit a wolf (the grid's spacing is a guess; no harness step clicks a world position).
# Coordinates are the 800x600 layout. The game runs ~5.5x faster (10 ms per world update).
