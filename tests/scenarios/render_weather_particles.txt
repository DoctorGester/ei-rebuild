# R59: the rain's and the snow's particles at 4:3, 16:9 and 21:9 (docs/notes/renderer.md section 12, "Weather").
# Zone1 in rain by day and the snow map gz11k in snow, each at 1440x1080, 1920x1080 and 2520x1080 (windowsize): a
# frozen frame against the same without the particles (they cover a share of the frame at every width), and a burst of
# four frames 16 ms apart (frametime 16: a real frame's step, about a third of a world update) to look at how the
# particles move from frame to frame. Frames only (goldens hold none); the captures to compare before and after a
# change: rain_4x3, rain_16x9, rain_21x9, snow_4x3, snow_16x9, snow_21x9 and their bursts (*_burst_1 .. 4), each kind
# at night (rain_night, snow_night: the particles in the scene's light) and at 3840x2160 (rain_4k, snow_4k).
render checkall
resolution 1440x1080
fullscreen 0
reject Script error
timeout 60
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
waitfor 15 IsUnitBlocked(GetLeader())
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 15 battle
console @FixWorldTime(12)
console @SetWeather("rain")
wait 6000
move 400 20
camera 33 64 0 20 38
frames 5

freeze
capture rain_4x3
weatherparticles off
capture rain_4x3_none
weatherparticles on
unfreeze
diffcapture rain_4x3 rain_4x3_none 0.002 0.2
frametime 16
capture rain_4x3_burst 4
frametime 0

windowsize 1920 1080
