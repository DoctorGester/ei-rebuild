# An AddMob'd .mob with a .lua beside it, loaded into zone 1's mission script while it runs (docs/notes/mods.md,
# "Scripts in Lua"; tools/make_lua_mod.py addmob): Script::LoadLua loads the .lua into the zone's scheduler in place
# of the .mob's script (the .mob, one of zone 1's map objects renumbered by make_trap_mob.py, has none). Its
# WorldScript prints a line with two of the zone's globals (Heroes, Ogre: the zone's script set them), starts a definition of its own that prints when its count reaches 3, and counts every 20 ticks into the game
# variable l8b.count. The game is saved and loaded: the count goes on from the saved coroutine (a restarted one would
# count from 1 again), and the zone's script with it.
# prepare: make_trap_mob.py --zone zone1 --kind B001 --first-id 30200 --count 1 --out l8b_addmob.mob
# prepare: make_lua_mod.py addmob
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitline 15 Saved savea
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
wait 2000
console @AddMob("..\saves\l8b_addmob.mob")
waitline 10 L8b: the AddMob'd Lua runs: heroes
waitline 10 L8b: the count reached 3
check IsAlive(GetObject(30200))
waitfor 20 IsGreater(GSGetVar(0,"l8b.count"),10)

key esc
waitwindow 10 escmenu
click 395 120
waitwindow 10 saveload
clickregion 0
waitline 15 Saved save1
wait 300
load save1
waitwindow 30 battle
waitfor 20 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
# Fewer than 10 counts' ticks after the load: a count started again would be below 10.
wait 100
check IsGreater(GSGetVar(0,"l8b.count"),10)
waitfor 20 IsGreater(GSGetVar(0,"l8b.count"),16)
dump loaded_save1
check IsAlive(GetLeader())
report
quit
