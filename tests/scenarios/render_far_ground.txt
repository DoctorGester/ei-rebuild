# The far ground (R62; docs/notes/renderer.md, "The view's depth"): pitched up, the ground ended in a hard edge where
# the camera's projection put its far plane, 100 units along the view, and the hills behind it were missing (the
# user's 4K frame). The projection has no far plane now. zone1 at 1920x1080 at the settings' least pitch (30 degrees,
# the most the player can pitch up) and farthest zoom (50): from the south-west corner looking north-east over the
# shore, and from the map's middle looking south and north. "groundreach" measures each frame's ground through the
# camera's clip planes: no run of it may be cut by the view's far side (71-108 of 451-519 were before), and from the
# corner and looking south it reaches past 150 units (121 before). Frames for the eye (build/captures/r62).

resolution 1920x1080

waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 15 battle

groundreach on

freeze
