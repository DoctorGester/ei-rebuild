# R44: the window resized in the middle of a zone changes the rendering resolution (app::SetResolution;
# docs/notes/renderer.md, "Resolution changes"): the frame, the layout (4:3 part, widescreen margins, the axis
# scales), the camera's viewport, the interface's fonts and surfaces and the battle screen's panels follow, the zone
# goes on. Windowed at 800x600 (4:3), then 1280x720 (wider: the panels move out to the edges by 134 layout units),
# then 1000x800 (narrower than 4:3: the layout letterboxed in the 1000x750 part, the world over the whole window),
# with the Esc menu open over the battle screen (the screen built again under it), then 800x600 again. Captures to
# look at: r4x3, wide, narrow_menu, narrow, back (each at its window's size).
render checkall
resolution 800x600
fullscreen 0
timeout 60
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 15 battle
move 400 300
waitpresent 10
freeze
capture r4x3
unfreeze

# Wider than 4:3.
windowsize 1280 720
waitpresent 10
frames 5
expectlog Resolution: 1280x720 (was 800x600)
expectlog Interface layout: 1280x720, 4:3 part 960x720, margins 160, 0 pixels (134 layout units)
waitwindow 10 battle
move 400 300
waitpresent 10
freeze
capture wide
unfreeze

# Narrower than 4:3, under the Esc menu.
key esc
waitwindow 10 escmenu
windowsize 1000 800
waitpresent 10
frames 5
expectlog Resolution: 1000x800 (was 1280x720)
expectlog Interface layout: 1000x800, 4:3 part 1000x750, margins 0, 25 pixels (0 layout units)
freeze
capture narrow_menu
unfreeze
key esc
waitwindow 10 battle
move 400 300
waitpresent 10
freeze
capture narrow
unfreeze

# Back to 4:3: the panels where they were.
windowsize 800 600
waitpresent 10
frames 5
expectlog Resolution: 800x600 (was 1000x800)
waitwindow 10 battle
move 400 300
waitpresent 10
freeze
capture back
unfreeze
report
quit
