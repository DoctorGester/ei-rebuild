# The showcase island's own AI region graph (MF13d; docs/notes/ai.md, "Built graphs"): the mod's zone "showcase" has
# its map from the folder examples/showcase_mod/Maps/showcase/ and no .mob, so its region graph is built from the AI
# map when the zone loads (64 x 48 blocks: the island's 8 x 6 sectors).
# - The hero walks from the party's landing (54, 138) past the golem's post and over the valley's hills to (130, 80),
#   about 96 units (some 70 s at a walk): a long path, planned as a chain of the graph's regions and a local search a
#   leg. The golem and its shards have their AI modes cleared (UMClear: they stand).
# - Saved on the way (x past 90) and played on to the end point. The save holds the built graph as its difference from
#   zeros (the zone has no graph file: ReadGraphBase); loaded, the hero stands where it was saved
#   and walks to the end point again on the save's graph (none is built at a save's load).
# prepare: make_showcase_mod.py
mod harness_saves\showcase_mod
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob

newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
waitwindow 15 battle
zone showcase 0
waitzone 30 showcase
waitline 20 Showcase mod: the island loads
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
waitwindow 10 battle
expectlog ai graph: built for 64 x 48 blocks
console @GodMode(0,1)
console @UMClear(GetObject(4001))
console @UMClear(GetObject(4002))
console @UMClear(GetObject(4003))
check IsLess(DistanceUnitPoint(GetLeader(),54,138),8)

console @MoveToPoint(GetLeader(),130,80)
waitfor 40 IsGreater(GetX(GetLeader()),90)
key esc
waitwindow 10 escmenu
click 395 120
waitwindow 10 saveload
clickregion 0
waitline 15 Saved save1
waitwindow 10 battle
waitfor 60 IsLess(DistanceUnitPoint(GetLeader(),130,80),3)
dump walked

wait 300
load save1
waitwindow 30 battle
waitfor 20 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
check IsGreater(GetX(GetLeader()),90)
check IsGreater(DistanceUnitPoint(GetLeader(),130,80),10)
console @MoveToPoint(GetLeader(),130,80)
waitfor 60 IsLess(DistanceUnitPoint(GetLeader(),130,80),3)
dump walked_loaded
report
quit
