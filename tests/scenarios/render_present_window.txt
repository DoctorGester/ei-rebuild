# R9c: the switch-over presenting through its own swap chain (docs/notes/renderer.md, section 9):
# the gamma ramp as a shader step, and a window that is not the frame's size. The game runs windowed
# at 800x600 (no display mode change in the switch-over; run_all runs a "resolution" scenario alone).
#
# Gamma: the options' gamma slider (group 0, row 12; its right arrow at 674,456 in the 800x600
# layout) held for 0.6 s (the slider scrolls while an arrow is held, 0065B360): the options
# window's Update (00650A30) calls SetGamma (00504A60), which hands the ramp to the new renderer;
# "waitpresent 10 gamma" needs a presented frame checked against it (verify
# build, at a grid of points, one to one). Cancel puts the old value back.
#
# Resize: the window resized to 1600x900, the frame is scaled into it with its aspect (1200x900 at
# x 200, black bars); the mouse steps reach the menus through the mapping (the harness aims at frame
# pixels through render::FrameToWindow, the game maps the messages back: present_window.h).
render new,present
resolution 800x600
fullscreen 0
reject Script error
waitmenu
waitpresent 10

clickregion 3
waitwindow 10 options
ldown 674 456
wait 600
lup 674 456
waitpresent 10 gamma
move 400 20
wait 300
freeze
capture options
unfreeze
clickregion 1
waitwindow 10 menu

# The window resized: the options again through the mapped clicks, then Cancel.
windowsize 1600 900
waitpresent 10
clickregion 3
waitwindow 10 options
waitpresent 10
clickregion 1
waitwindow 10 menu
report
quit
