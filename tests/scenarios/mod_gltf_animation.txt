# Animations as glTF (docs/notes/mods.md, "Animations as glTF"): a mod of our own, tools/make_mod_s19d.py anim, written
# into the run's harness_saves\s19d_mod, which "mod" makes the settings' Mod (either install). Its Res\figures\unhuma.glb
# is the hero's figure as ei-convert writes it with its idle "cidle18" twice as slow (its keys' times doubled, its
# extras dropped), and its Res\database\unhuma.adb.toml gives "cidle18" twice its frames. The glTF stands for the game's
# unhuma.anm: the figure manager's track of the hips has a key at every tick of the doubled time (211 for the game's
# 106), the other idles' as they were, the hero plays "cidle18" standing past the game's 105 frames (objplayed past),
# and the trace has the glTF read for the .anm.
# prepare: make_mod_s19d.py anim
mod harness_saves\s19d_mod
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob

newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 10 battle
objtrace start leader hd log
frames 120
objtrace stop idle
objplayed cidle18 past 105
modrecord track unhuma/cidle18/hp = rotations 211 positions 211 frames 0 slots 0
modrecord track unhuma/cidle02/hp = rotations 96 positions 96 frames 0 slots 0
modrecord anim unhuma/cidle18 = chance 50 frames 210 key 8058007f flags 32 moment 0
capture world
read mod Res\figures\unhuma.anm yes
report
quit
