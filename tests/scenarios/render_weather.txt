# R17c: the weather on the surfaces (docs/notes/renderer.md section 13, item 11b) at 1440x1080 by day. Zone1's start
# dry, a few seconds into rain, after a minute of rain (wet ground and objects, puddles, the drops' rings), half a
# minute after the rain stopped (drying), the sea in rain (rings and splashes); the snow map gz11k, entered with
# zone1's ground still wet (R17c1: dry at once), before the snow, five seconds and two minutes into it (the flakes
# seen, the cover settling over minutes), then a minute of rain (the cover melting). The rain's and the snow's
# particles: a frozen frame against the same without them (`weatherparticles off`, R53). `weathercheck` reads how wet
# the ground is and the snow's cover; its ranges are wide, as the game's rain grows and fades at its own pace
# (Rebirth's faster, and five minutes after SetWeather("clear") its ground is still a little wet). Frames only
# (goldens hold none).
# Only on the vanilla data: the Rebirth run reaches no code and checks nothing the vanilla run does not.
# installs: vanilla
render checkall
resolution 1440x1080
