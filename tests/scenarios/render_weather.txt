# R17c: the weather on the surfaces (docs/notes/renderer.md section 13, item 11b) at 1440x1080 by day. Zone1's start
# dry, a few seconds into rain, after a minute of rain (wet ground and objects, puddles, the drops' rings), half a minute
# after the rain stopped (drying), the sea in rain (rings and splashes); the snow map gz11k before and after two
# minutes of snow. `weathercheck` reads how wet the ground is and the snow's cover. Frames only (goldens hold none).
render checkall
resolution 1440x1080
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
waitfor 15 IsUnitBlocked(GetLeader())
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 15 battle
console @FixWorldTime(12)
console @SetWeather("clear")
waitpresent 10
move 400 20
frames 5
vanilla minimap 701 81 2.8 2.8
rebirth minimap 726 80.6 3.75 2.7
wait 300000
frames 5
freeze
weathercheck 0 0.05 0 0
capture weather_dry
unfreeze

capture weather_rain_minute_again
