# R45: a resolution picked in the options window under a zone (docs/notes/renderer.md, "Resolution changes"). Its
# display group's last row steps through the common sizes and the screen's own (Enter or a click steps on, Left /
# Right back and on); OK asks for it at the next main loop pass (app::ChooseResolution -> app::SetResolution) and,
# windowed, sizes the window's client area to it (platform::SetClientSize), so the present that follows asks for
# nothing and the frame is the window's size. Windowed at 800x600: the options from the Esc menu, 1280x720 picked
# (wider than 4:3: the battle screen's panels at the edges), OK (which closes the Esc menu too); then 1280x1024
# (narrower than 4:3). Captures to look at: picked (the row showing 1280 x 720), wide, narrow_options, narrow (each at
# its size).
render checkall
resolution 800x600
fullscreen 0
timeout 60
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 15 battle
move 400 300
frames 5

# The Esc menu's options (region 4), the display group (shown first), its last row (region 13 + 9).
key esc
waitwindow 10 escmenu
clickregion 4
waitwindow 10 options
clickregion 22
key right
key right
key right
key right
key left
frames 5
move 400 20
frames 5
freeze
capture picked
unfreeze
clickregion 0
waitwindow 10 battle
waitpresent 10
frames 5
expectlog present: the window's client area sized to 1280x720 (asked 1280x720)
expectlog Resolution: 1280x720 (was 800x600)
expectlog Interface layout: 1280x720, 4:3 part 960x720, margins 160, 0 pixels (134 layout units)
move 400 300
frames 5
freeze
capture wide
unfreeze

# 1280x1024 (narrower than 4:3: the layout letterboxed in the 1280x960 part), from the row's 1280 x 720 three on.
key esc
waitwindow 10 escmenu
clickregion 4
waitwindow 10 options
clickregion 22
key right
key right
key right
frames 5
move 400 20
frames 5
freeze
capture narrow_options
unfreeze
clickregion 0
waitwindow 10 battle
waitpresent 10
frames 5
expectlog present: the window's client area sized to 1280x1024 (asked 1280x1024)
expectlog Resolution: 1280x1024 (was 1280x720)
expectlog Interface layout: 1280x1024, 4:3 part 1280x960, margins 0, 32 pixels (0 layout units)
move 400 300
frames 5
freeze
capture narrow
unfreeze
report
quit
