# The HUD unit panel's figure (top left) through a path change and an animation switch (R19b; docs/notes/renderer.md
# section 14, "Objects"). At 8 ms frames the hero, standing, is sent west: its new path's start lies a little off its
# place, so the world draws it with a jump that fades over 150 ms (drawn::PoseOf), and its idle blends into the cross.
# The panel draws the same unit where the panel puts it; the world's fading jump once moved it there too, toward the
# top right ("lastdraw": the place its last draw used, the panel's). The panel's place must stay still while the
# world's walk moves (captures panel_*: the switch is between panel_1 and panel_2).
timeout 60
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
waitfor 15 IsUnitBlocked(GetLeader())
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
console @GodMode(0,1)
waitwindow 15 battle
move 400 300
check Not(IsUnitBlocked(GetLeader()))
console @MoveToPoint(GetLeader(),Add(GetX(GetLeader()),6),GetY(GetLeader()))
frames 100
frametime 8
frames 30
objtrace start leader lastdraw log
console @MoveToPoint(GetLeader(),Add(GetX(GetLeader()),-6),GetY(GetLeader()))
frames 4
capture panel_0
frames 2
capture panel_1
frames 2
capture panel_2
frames 4
capture panel_3
frames 30
objtrace stop panel
objtrace start leader -
frames 30
objtrace stop walk
camsteadier panel walk pan 0.05
frametime 0
check IsAlive(GetLeader())
report
quit
