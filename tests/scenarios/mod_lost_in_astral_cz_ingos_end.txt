# Lost in Astral's cut-scene bases on Ingos, cz3k and cz4k, and Jigran's cz1j (MOD2a; docs/notes/mods.md, "Lost in
# Astral played (MOD2)"), from the story's own state: the party Gipat (the hero Hero3 and the mercenary merc2g, member
# "merc2"). Each base's briefing is read to the end (talk) and the base leaves for the zone its script names (bz21k,
# then cz1j for cz4k, with the movie between, and gz31j for cz1j); the briefing's variable is checked there. The bases
# before them are mod_lost_in_astral_cz_ingos (H70). One digest record, on arrival in bz21k (a small zone; the others
# are checked by their variables). On the vanilla data only the menu runs.

# installs: rebirth
mod starter\Mods\LostInAstral

waitmenu
digest off

rebirth newgame
rebirth waitfor 20 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
rebirth waitline 20 Saved savea
rebirth waitwindow 15 battle

rebirth console @CreateParty(0,"Gipat")
rebirth console @AddUnitToParty(0,"Gipat::Hero","Hero3")
rebirth console @AddUnitToParty(0,"Gipat::merc2","merc2g")
rebirth console @SetCurrentParty(0,"Gipat")
rebirth console @RedeployParty(0)
rebirth waitfor 10 IsAlive(GetObjectByName("merc2"))

rebirth zone cz3k 0
rebirth waitzone 25 cz3k
rebirth waitdialogue 30 3
rebirth talk 40
rebirth waitzone 40 bz21k
rebirth check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"b.Vor.brief_250"),2)
rebirth digest

rebirth zone cz4k 0
rebirth waitzone 25 cz4k
rebirth waitdialogue 30 3
rebirth talk 40
rebirth waitzone 40 cz1j
rebirth waitdialogue 30 3
rebirth talk 40
rebirth waitzone 40 gz31j

rebirth check IsAlive(GetObjectByName("merc2"))
