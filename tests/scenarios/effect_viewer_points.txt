# The effect viewer's points of the figure (ei-effects CastingFire; docs/modding.md, "The effect viewer"; MF15k5): the
# Place window's list (ViewerScene::Points) and the effect put on the game's points and on a bone by name. Checked:
# - CastingFire's own `part = "effect_point"` (the game's way: OnPart, the host's point) sits at the casting hand, rh3;
# - the game's points picked by their file names (head, above_head 1 higher) and the feet the effects read (ll3);
# - a bone by name (lh3) followed through the idle, the walk round the circle and the run: the source's carrier point
#   is the bone's point at every check, and it moves with it;
# - captures: the casting hand standing, the left hand walking.
# exe: ei-effects
# arguments: CastingFire
wait 1200
effectviewer camera 6
effectviewer points
effectviewer expect particles 5
effectviewer expect at rh3
capture viewer_points_hand
frames 3

effectviewer place point head
effectviewer expect at hd
effectviewer place point above_head
frames 2
effectviewer expect at hd 1
effectviewer place point left_foot
frames 2
effectviewer expect at ll3

effectviewer place point lh3
wait 400
effectviewer expect at lh3
effectviewer mark
wait 600
effectviewer expect at lh3
effectviewer expect moved 0.005
effectviewer figure walk on
effectviewer camera 10
wait 900
effectviewer expect at lh3
effectviewer mark
wait 600
effectviewer expect at lh3
effectviewer expect moved 0.3
capture viewer_points_walk
frames 3
effectviewer figure run on
wait 900
effectviewer expect at lh3
effectviewer figure walk off
effectviewer figure run off
effectviewer place point file
frames 2
effectviewer expect at rh3
report
quit
