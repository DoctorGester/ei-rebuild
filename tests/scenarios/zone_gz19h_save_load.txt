# Saving and loading on the Portal zone, gz19h (map.txt "## Portal": zone19.mpr / zone19.mob, the largest map and
# .mob of the game; Suslanger's quest q72h "Портал"; ticket PF4). The user remembers it as the slowest zone to save
# and load. From the new game in zone1 the "zone" step goes through exit 0, "To Pritown" (deploy 2 428 14 437); the
# zone entry autosaves into savea (`waitexpect` looks only past the last `expect` or `waitexpect`). Then F5
# quick-saves into saveq, and the quick save and the autosave are loaded back. EI_PROFILE=1 shows where the time
# goes (docs/notes/harness.md, "Timing runs").
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitline 15 Saved savea
expect Saved savea
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
waitfor 15 IsUnitBlocked(GetLeader())
zone gz19h 0
waitzone 30 gz19h
waitexpect 20 Saved savea
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @GodMode(0,1)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
check IsLess(DistanceUnitPoint(GetLeader(),8,432.5),10)
waitwindow 15 battle
wait 500
key f5
waitexpect 20 Saved saveq
wait 500
key f8
waitexpect 30 Loaded saveq
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
dump loaded_saveq
wait 500
load savea
waitzone 30 gz19h
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
check IsLess(DistanceUnitPoint(GetLeader(),8,432.5),10)
wait 500
check IsAlive(GetLeader())
report
quit
