# MF17d: a scripted spell through the trader, the camp and the HUD (docs/notes/items_spells.md, "Scripted spells";
# tools/make_spell_mod.py's "mf17d_bolt", place 42, icon 100).
# - The trader: Ingos' camp (class 3) opens with the mod's scroll and prototype in its magic stock (the record's sold_by
#   and its [[scroll_recipe]]; campstock reads the class template's stock).
# - The camp: a spell container of it and its prototype (the spell maker's loot item, "prototype.mf17d_bolt") given;
#   the magic page draws them with the mod's pictures (Res\textures\spell0100.png, prototype0100.png) and puts the
#   container on the hero (campputon, as in spells_hero), so the HUD's spell column holds healing, 0x0C and the mod's
#   spell (hudstate: prototype 42 in slot 2).
# - The HUD: its key (3) and a click on a wolf at +3 cast it as the hero's spells are cast (OrderCast, mana, the cast
#   animation, CastSpell); the zone's server state runs the file: OnCast's line, the caster and the target in game
#   variables, the wolf's blow; the cast ends after 60 ticks. (The use at once, Ctrl and the key, aims an offensive
#   spell at the nearest unit the hero sees: here it finds none for the game's lightning either, so it is not used.)
# prepare: make_spell_mod.py
mod harness_saves\spell_mod
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
waitfor 15 IsUnitBlocked(GetLeader())
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
console @GodMode(0,1)
console @GiveSkill(GetLeader(),"elemental",5)
console @GiveIntelligence(GetLeader(),60)

# The trader's stock.
screen 5 3
waitwindow 15 camp
campstock 3 scroll mf17d_bolt 1
campstock 3 prototype mf17d_bolt 1
key esc
waitwindow 15 battle
waitfor 10 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)

# The camp's magic page: the container and the prototype drawn with the mod's pictures, the container put on.
console @GiveItem(0,"spell container[mf17d_bolt]")
frames 5
console @GiveItem(0,"prototype.mf17d_bolt")
frames 5
screen 5
waitwindow 15 camp
clickregion 5
wait 500
campputon 3008 1
frames 3
read mod Res\textures\spell0100.mmp yes
read mod Res\textures\prototype0100.mmp yes
key esc
waitwindow 15 battle
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
console @GSSetVar(0,"hx",GetX(GetLeader()))
console @GSSetVar(0,"hy",GetY(GetLeader()))

# The hero's portrait: selects it and shows its spells.
click 400 560
frames 3
hudstate

console @AddUnitToServer("BoltWolf","WolfTutor","BoltWolf",Add(GSGetVar(0,"hx"),3),GSGetVar(0,"hy"),0)
waitfor 10 IsAlive(GetObjectByName("BoltWolf"))
console @BlockUnit(GetObjectByName("BoltWolf"),1)
wait 500

# The mod's spell key, then the wolf.
waitunit 10 BoltWolf
check Not(IsUnitBlocked(GetLeader()))
key 3
frames 2
clickunit BoltWolf
waitline 15 MF17d: OnCast mf17d_bolt by
check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"mf17d.caster"),GetObjectID(GetLeader()))
check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"mf17d.target"),GetObjectID(GetObjectByName("BoltWolf")))
frames 2
clientbolts 1
waitline 10 MF17d: OnEnd finished at tick 60
check IsAlive(GetLeader())
report
quit
