# R49a: a creature that joins the party through AddUnitToParty keeps its texture in the next zone. The original's party
# record made by AddUnitToParty (0065F5F0, 006621F0) left the skin texture names (texts 3 and 4) empty, so the creature
# came back untextured after a zone change (zone4hadagans' DrPlen). WorldServer::TakeLookFromUnit copies the map
# unit's names into the record. tools/make_trap_mob.py copies zone1's first textured wolf (Wolf00 GoblinAxeBag00) into
# joinwolf.mob with the id of the text key "JoinWolf" (1007305800), beside the hero's start; the scenario finds it by
# name (script numbers are floats) and AddUnitToParty names it. The party is quick-saved and loaded back (the record's
# names go through the save) and leaves for gz2g; there the client's joined wolf must hold the map unit's texture names
# (the harness's "unitskins" step; without the fix both are empty and the unit draws untextured).
# prepare: make_trap_mob.py --zone zone1 --kind BBBB0000 --with Wolf00 --count 1 --first-id 1007305800 --at 56 138 --out joinwolf.mob
render checkall
timeout 60
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
waitfor 15 IsUnitBlocked(GetLeader())
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),1)
console @GodMode(0,1)
console @FixWorldTime(12)
waitwindow 15 battle
console @AddMob("..\saves\joinwolf.mob")
waitfor 10 IsAlive(GetObjectByName("JoinWolf"))
console @AddUnitToParty(GetPlayer(GetLeader()),"JoinWolf","WolfTutorWOLoot")
wait 1000
key f5
waitline 20 Saved saveq
key f8
waitline 30 Loaded saveq
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
check IsAlive(GetObjectByName("JoinWolf"))

zone gz2g 0
waitzone 30 gz2g
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),1)
console @GodMode(0,1)
console @FixWorldTime(12)
waitwindow 15 battle
check IsAlive(GetObjectByName("JoinWolf"))
waitunit 10 JoinWolf
unitskins JoinWolf Wolf00 GoblinAxeBag00
report
quit
