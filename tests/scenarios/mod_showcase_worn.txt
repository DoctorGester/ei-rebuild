# The showcase mod's hammer in the hero's hand (examples/showcase_mod; docs/notes/mods.md, "Worn models"): the hammer
# (club number 9, which no figure of the game has) brings its own worn model, Res\figures\hm_09.glb: its part
# rh3.club09 under the bone rh3.axe00 the game's clubs hang from. The part joins the hero's figure set (unhuma: its link
# list, records and bones), so the hero's skeleton has a bone of its name from the start and no unit wears it yet
# (unitpart 0). The hero takes the hammer (GiveUnitWeapon puts it in the hand); the client loads the part as it loads a
# game's club (ShowWeapon: LoadPart, the bone of its name carries it), and the hero wears it (unitpart 1, logged with
# the bone's parent and place). Capture: the hero with the hammer, near (worn), its head in the weapons' strip of the
# hero's second texture (Res\redress\unhumahm_09.gr.0.png).
# prepare: make_showcase_mod.py
mod harness_saves\showcase_mod
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob

newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
waitwindow 15 battle
zone showcase 0
waitzone 30 showcase
waitline 20 Showcase mod: the island loads
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
waitwindow 10 battle
console @GodMode(0,1)
unitpart rh3.club09 0
console @MoveToPoint(GetLeader(),56,136)
waitfor 15 IsLess(DistanceUnitPoint(GetLeader(),56,136),1)
console @GiveUnitWeapon(GetLeader(),"showcase hammer.granite",0)
frames 30
unitpart rh3.club09 1
camera 56 136 20 5 40
frames 5
capture worn

read mod Res\figures\hm_09.glb yes
read mod Res\redress\unhumahm_09.gr.0.mmp yes
report
quit
