# R46: the windows over a zone drawn again after a resolution change (docs/notes/renderer.md, "Resolution changes"):
# each open windowed at 1280x720 or 1000x800, the window resized to the other size while it is open, then closed and
# opened again at that size: the frame after the resize is the frame of the window opened at that size (samecapture;
# the game's clocks held from the resize to the second capture, so the animated portrait and minimap are the same; a
# window opened while they are held keeps its own pulses and fades at their start: the objectives' quest markers and
# map are let differ in 3% of the pixels, the tutorial's pulsing frames by up to 130 a channel, which a frame left at
# the old edge exceeds).
# The screen copies behind the windows are taken again at the new size (Interface3D::RetakeBackdrops: the Esc menu's
# greyed one, the help window's), the zone tutorial's frames follow the battle screen's panels to the new edges
# (HelpWindow::LayoutChanged). Windows: the Esc menu, the save window over it (resized only: it slides in), the
# objectives, the zone tutorial "zt4" (frames at the right panels), the camp's inventory and trade pages, the global
# map. The hero is blocked, the server paused under the windows; the mouse stays off the edges (no edge pan).
# Captures to look at: <window>_wide / _narrow (after the resize) and their _fresh twins, save_wide, camp_narrow,
# map_narrow.
render checkall
resolution 1280x720
fullscreen 0
timeout 60
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
console @GodMode(0,1)
console @GSSetVar(0,"q.gz1g.q0g",1)
waitfor 5 IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.gz1g.q0g"),1)
waitwindow 15 battle
move 400 300
frames 5

# The Esc menu over its greyed backdrop, resized to 1000x800.
key esc
waitwindow 10 escmenu
frames 5
freeze
windowsize 1000 800
waitpresent 10
frames 5
expectlog Resolution: 1000x800 (was 1280x720)
capture esc_narrow
key esc
waitwindow 10 battle
frames 5
key esc
waitwindow 10 escmenu
frames 5
capture esc_narrow_fresh
unfreeze
samecapture esc_narrow esc_narrow_fresh
