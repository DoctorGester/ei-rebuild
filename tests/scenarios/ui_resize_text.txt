# P9: text stays sharp through window resizes. The interface is drawn each frame at the current size (no window
# canvases stretched from their last copy), so the options window and its help page, captured at 800x600, then
# after shrinking and growing the window several times (640x480, 1600x1200, 700x525, 1280x960) and back at 800x600,
# are the same frame. Captures to look at: options, options_large, help, help_back (and the others at their sizes).
resolution 800x600
fullscreen 0
timeout 60
reject Script error
waitmenu
clickregion 3
waitwindow 10 options
move 400 20
frames 5
freeze
capture options
unfreeze

windowsize 640 480
waitpresent 10
frames 5
windowsize 1600 1200
waitpresent 10
frames 5
freeze
capture options_large
unfreeze
windowsize 700 525
waitpresent 10
frames 5
windowsize 1280 960
waitpresent 10
frames 5
windowsize 800 600
waitpresent 10
frames 5
expectlog Resolution: 800x600 (was 1280x960)
move 400 20
frames 5
freeze
capture options_back
unfreeze
samecapture options options_back
