# The showcase mod (examples/showcase_mod; docs/notes/mods.md, "The showcase mod"): a mod made only of our own files,
# copied by tools/make_showcase_mod.py into the run's harness_saves\showcase_mod, which "mod" makes the settings' Mod.
# Checked:
# - Res\texts\map.toml, Maps\showcase\, Maps\showcase.mob.toml, Maps\showcase.lua: the mod's own zone "showcase"
#   (entered from a new game with "zone"; mod_showcase_zone walks there through its exit): its map from the folder
#   alone and its objects from the TOML alone (no .mpr, no .mob: its AI graph built at the load), its script runs
#   (the console line) and gives the quest (q.showcase.showcase_golem 1) and the Chain Spark's spell container
#   (mod_showcase_spell casts it); the golem's two shards (4002, 4003) stand by it and join the fight;
# - Res\database\units.udb.toml, scgolem.adb.toml, Res\figures\scgolem.glb, Res\textures\scgolem00.png: the golem
#   (map object 4001) stands, sees the hero coming, runs to it and attacks (objplayed: its glTF animations by the
#   animation list's names), and the hero (god mode) breaks it in the fight;
# - the quest: the golem's death finishes it (q.showcase.showcase_golem 2, the message log's lines), and the party has the
#   mod's hammer from items.idb.toml at granite's damage (4.0 to 6.0 times 3.6) and its boots;
# - captures: the fight (the golem on the island's ground, the minimap the mod's picture), the golem broken with the
#   quest's lines, and the camp's inventory with the hammer's and the boots' inventory models (initwehm9.glb,
#   initarbt20.glb) in their textures (Res\redress).
# prepare: make_showcase_mod.py
mod harness_saves\showcase_mod
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob

newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
waitwindow 15 battle
zone showcase 0
waitzone 30 showcase
waitline 20 Showcase mod: the island loads
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
check IsAlive(GetObject(4001))
check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.showcase.showcase_golem"),1)
waitwindow 10 battle
console @GodMode(0,1)
console @MoveToPoint(GetLeader(),GetX(GetObject(4001)),GetY(GetObject(4001)))
objtrace start #4001 - log
waitfor 30 IsLess(DistanceUnitUnit(GetLeader(),GetObject(4001)),5)
frames 20
camera 76 118 0 12 50
frames 5
capture fight
waitline 40 Showcase mod: the golem is broken
check IsDead(GetObject(4001))
check IsEqual(GSGetVar(0,"q.showcase.showcase_golem"),2)
modweapon showcase hammer 14.4 21.6
frames 40
objtrace stop golem
objplayed crun
objplayed uattack01
capture broken

screen 5 1
waitwindow 15 camp
frames 10
capture camp

read mod Res\texts\map.toml yes
read mod Maps\showcase.lua yes
read mod Maps\showcase.mob.toml yes
read mod Maps\showcase\heights.png yes
read mod Maps\showcase\tiles.png yes
read mod Res\textures\showcase000.mmp yes
read mod Res\textures\showcasemap.mmp yes
read mod Res\textures\scgolem00.mmp yes
read mod Res\figures\scgolem.mod yes
read mod Res\figures\scgolem.anm yes
read mod Res\figures\initwehm9weapon.fig yes
read mod Res\figures\initarbt20armor.fig yes
read mod Res\redress\unhumahm_09.gr.0.mmp yes
read mod Res\redress\unhumabt_20.le.0.mmp yes
report
quit
