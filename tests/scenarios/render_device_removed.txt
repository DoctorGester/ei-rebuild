# R9c5: a real removal of the new renderer's device (docs/notes/renderer.md, section 9). "removable"
# (harness build only) makes the Direct3D 11 device through D3D11On12 on a Direct3D 12 device, and
# "removedevice" removes that device (ID3D12Device5::RemoveDevice: this process only, no driver reset):
# the swap chain's Present and GetDeviceRemovedReason report DXGI_ERROR_DEVICE_REMOVED as after a
# driver reset, and the renderer releases everything and makes a new (removable) device, the frame
# target, the swap chain, the window surfaces and the textures again. render_device_lost does the
# same with the loss only reported (render::LoseDevice) on the hardware device players get. At one
# frozen moment the frame before the removal and the frame after it must match, in the main menu, in
# the world and in the camp (textures loaded on the second device).
# no original: vanilla rebirth (removedevice needs our renderer; S21a)
# Its golden is the image's own (golden.py record --image, S20a): the world it plays does not follow the renderer.
render new,present,removable,checkall
reject Script error
waitmenu
waitpresent 10
console thingamabob

# The main menu.
move 400 20
wait 500
freeze
capture menu
removedevice 10
waitpresent 10
capture menu_again
unfreeze
samecapture menu menu_again

# The hero on zone1, unblocked and standing still, the camera settled on him.
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 10 battle
move 400 60
wait 1000
freeze
capture world
removedevice 10
waitpresent 10
capture world_again
unfreeze
samecapture world world_again

# The camp (screen 5), loaded on the new device, and a removal there; Esc returns to the game.
screen 5
waitwindow 15 camp
move 400 20
wait 300
freeze
capture camp
removedevice 10
waitpresent 10
capture camp_again
unfreeze
samecapture camp camp_again
key esc
waitwindow 15 battle
waitpresent 10
report
quit
