# Companions: a player's other saved hero walks with its hero as a companion, added and sent away only in the base
# zone (where quests are taken; GameFlow::InMultiplayerBase), kept for the whole game. This game makes two heroes,
# "comp" and then "host" (the one it plays), hosts with the create-server screen's companions switch on (region 30)
# and the peer (peer/net_pair_companion.txt) joins. In the base:
# - The HUD's companions button opens the companions window; its first row is "comp" (region 2), OK (region 0) asks
#   the server for it (ClientMessage::AddCompanion): the party holds two records and the companion stands beside the
#   hero. The sheriff's second topic (z3q2_1, as net_pair_zone) picks the map; out through the gate into z3q2, where
#   the companion comes back by itself (PlayerClient::RestoreCompanions).
# In z3q2 (outside the base):
# - The button is hidden and F3 opens nothing; an add and a removal sent past the window are refused by the server
#   (the party keeps its two records).
# - The companion fights: it kills the hostile unit nearest it (the harness's hit), so the kill's experience is
#   shared among the zone's heroes, the companion's share among them; then it is killed itself (the hero's death
#   penalty on its experience, PlayerServer::CompanionDied). Then the hero dies (as net_host_death): the hero's rule,
#   and the party, the dead companion standing again, comes back together at the zone's entrance.
# - The peer quits (the exits stay closed while another player is in the game). Back out through exit 1 (as
#   net_host_death), the hero and the companion selected: the party goes home and the client writes the companion's record
#   into comp's own file (SavedParties::ReturnCompanions); heroxp reads that file from the disk.
# Back in the base: out through the gate again, comp comes back by itself (companions last the game) and kills; home
# again, F3 and OK on its row send it away (ClientMessage::RemoveCompanion); its record is in comp's file.
