# P12: the chase camera ([camera] ChaseCamera, GameOptions::SetChaseCamera: Rebirth's starter's "Камера Чейза"
# preset) switched on in the options window (the sens group's row 8) and used with the player's keys. Off, Ctrl + the
# camera keys tilt within the usual 30..60 degrees; on, from straight down (90) to up past level (-45), and the zoom
# keys bring the camera right to its focus (distance 0) and out again; the wheel does the same (Camera::ZoomBy, from
# 1 when nearer). Looking up, the up key pans forward (Camera::Pan along the ground: the camera's own Y axis, which
