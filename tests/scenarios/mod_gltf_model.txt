# Models as glTF (docs/notes/mods.md, "Models as glTF"): a mod of our own, tools/make_mod_s19d.py gltf, written into
# the run's harness_saves\s19d_mod, which "mod" makes the settings' Mod (either install). Its Res\figures\unhuma.glb is
# the hero's figure as another tool would leave it: no mesh or scene extras (so the loader numbers the slots by value
# and fits the bounds; the nodes keep their bones' versions) and 1.5 times as large. It stands for the game's
# unhuma.mod and unhuma.bon: the hero in the portrait panel is larger than in mod_png_texture's (the capture), and the
# trace has the glTF read for the .mod and the .bon.
# prepare: make_mod_s19d.py gltf
mod harness_saves\s19d_mod
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob

newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 10 battle
move 400 60
wait 1000
freeze
capture world
unfreeze
read mod Res\figures\unhuma.mod yes
read mod Res\figures\unhuma.bon yes
report
quit
