# R52: ground prints in the shadows (docs/notes/renderer.md section 13, item 10). Blood splats, footprints and scorch
# marks are flat geometry laid on the ground (ground_print.cpp) and blended over it; they took none of the shadows,
# so a blood splat under a tree showed sunlit on the darkened ground. Now they are darkened as the ground under them
# (the sun map, the clouds, the point shadows; mesh.hlsl's ground_shade). In gz2g's forest at noon the hero walks
# (footprints) and eight wolves die around him (their death blood) and are taken off the map; frozen: the frame and the shadow alone ("shadowview
# on": white lit, black fully shadowed), each with the prints and without them ("decals off"). shadowcheck requires
# every print to sample the map; the shadow alone with the prints matches it without them (a print shows the
# ground's shade, not its own colour: before the fix the blood showed red there), while the frames differ (the
# prints are drawn; printlight: their brightness over the ground's under them). Then at night, and at night under a
# point light.

# At night (R52a): the prints are lit as the ground under them, so they stand against it as by day (printlight: their
# brightness over the ground's under them). The game's own colours are unlit: white footprints and scorch marks,
# blood re-lit with a grey every 10 s.
console @FixWorldTime(1)
frames 5
freeze
capture night
decals off
capture night_bare

# With a script point light right of the hero: the prints take its light as the ground
# does (before R52a the blood kept its last re-lighting's grey: 0.87, darker than by day), and in its point shadows
# lose it as the ground does.

# Under a spell's light (R52c): spell, missile and quest lights are specular, which the ground adds again after its
# texture (terrain_ground.hlsl: t x max(other, spec) + spec); a print takes the same added light over its colour, so it is
# not darker than its ground by that light times its alpha. The lamp goes; a wolf by the hero dies (its blood), then
# lightning strikes a second wolf next to it and leaves a light at its eyes for 7 updates. printlight: 1.06 (the
# blood under the light without the added light: 1.03). The ratio is over every pixel that differs between the two
# frames, which holds the lightning bolt (it is not frozen: it differs between the captures) and the blood far from the
# light, so it is a bound on the whole, not on the lit print. The capture is taken 6 frames after the cast (R37e4e: with `wait 150`,
# which lets one frame pass, it fell on the bolt's first frame and held it or not by where the client generator stood). Rebirth's gz2g gives 1.03 with the added light and 1.00
# without it (R16j3a: the same gain of 0.036 as vanilla's 1.06 over 1.03; its lit ground is brighter, 123 against 111,
# and its bolt differs less between the frames), so its floor is lower.
console @DeletePointLight(7)
console @AddUnitToServer("S1","WolfTutor","S1",Add(GSGetVar(0,"hx"),2),Add(GSGetVar(0,"hy"),1),0)
waitfor 10 IsAlive(GetObjectByName("S1"))
console @InflictDamage(GetObjectByName("S1"),100000)
wait 3000
console @RemoveUnitFromServer(GetObjectByName("S1"))

waitfor 10 IsAlive(GetObjectByName("S2"))
