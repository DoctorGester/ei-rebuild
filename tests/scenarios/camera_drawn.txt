# The drawn view and the game's state (R35c; docs/notes/renderer.md section 14, "Checks"). At a fixed frame time
# of 8 ms (a 120 Hz screen's frames on the virtual clock) the world's draw shows the camera's pose less the part of
# its last tick's step the frame has not reached (camera::DrawnView); the harness's default pass of five ticks draws
# the camera's own pose, so this is the one golden scenario where the two differ. The hero walks while the camera
# pans and turns, and clickunit clicks a wolf where the game's picking finds it in the drawn frame (the attack
# order: the hero walks to it and fights). The golden is the original's (which draws the camera's own pose): what
# the drawn view feeds (the Shown flags, the visible and shadow lists, picking) must not reach the world's state.
reject Script error
timeout 120
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),1)
console @GodMode(0,1)
console @GSSetVar(0,"hx",GetX(GetLeader()))
console @GSSetVar(0,"hy",GetY(GetLeader()))
waitwindow 15 battle
move 400 300
console @AddUnitToServer("DrawnWolf","WolfTutor","DrawnWolf",Add(GSGetVar(0,"hx"),2),GSGetVar(0,"hy"),0)
waitfor 10 IsAlive(GetObjectByName("DrawnWolf"))
console @BlockUnit(GetObjectByName("DrawnWolf"),1)
waitunit 10 DrawnWolf
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
frametime 8
frames 30

# The hero walks west while the camera pans; a click on the wolf mid-pan.
console @MoveToPoint(GetLeader(),Sub(GSGetVar(0,"hx"),3),GSGetVar(0,"hy"))
keydown up
frames 25
clickunit DrawnWolf
frames 25
keyup up
frames 30

# The camera turns while the hero fights; a second click mid-turn, with Ctrl (the same order).
hold ctrl
keydown left
frames 30
clickunit DrawnWolf l
frames 40
keyup left
release ctrl
frames 60
frametime 0
frames 5
check IsAlive(GetLeader())
console #HP(GetObjectByName("DrawnWolf"))
report
quit
