# Frame captures for the renderer work (docs/notes/renderer.md, "Frame capture"): a few fixed
# screens written to frames\<name>.bmp in the run's output folder. Compare two runs with
#   python tools\compare_frames.py build\verify\runs\frames_basic-vanilla <other run folder>
# Around each capture the game's clocks and the camera are frozen (no world, client or camera
# update, no clock-driven animation) and the mouse is parked away from buttons and units; each
# capture skips two presented frames. world and world_again come from the same freeze and must match
# (samecapture; a difference means something on screen moves while frozen).
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob

# The main menu.
move 400 20
wait 500
freeze
capture menu
unfreeze

# The hero on zone1, unblocked and standing still, the camera settled on him.
newgame
speed 10
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 10 battle
move 400 60
wait 1000
freeze
capture world
capture world_again
unfreeze
samecapture world world_again
