# The quick save's picture (R13; docs/notes/renderer.md section 6, "Save / load screen's pictures"):
# the battle screen's F5 (action 0x35) saves into "saveq" through 006570E0 with no frame drawn first (DL 0), so
# CaptureScreen (005FD3D0) copies the screen between frames, before the next frame's draws: the last
# frame, as the original's render target holds it (the image once cleared its frame there and saved
# plain blue). checksave compares the save's shot.sav with a capture of the same frozen moment (on
# Rebirth's data the copy is the 800x600 layout's centred 4:3 rectangle: WideScreen); then the Load
# window shows the pictures (saveq selected, then savea).
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitline 15 Saved savea
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 15 battle
move 400 20
frames 5
freeze
capture world
key f5
waitline 15 Saved saveq
vanilla checksave saveq world 32 0.02
rebirth checksave saveq world centre 32 0.02
unfreeze
frames 5
