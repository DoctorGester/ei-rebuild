# The save / load screen's pictures (docs/notes/renderer.md sections 6 and 7): after the new game's autosave
# (savea, with info.sav and shot.sav), the escape menu's Save window (the new save's picture, drawn by FillEntries
# into its texture on the GPU from the escape menu's copy of the screen), a save into it (SaveGame writes shot.sav
# from the copy the window kept), then the Load window (ShowPicture draws a shot.sav into the picture texture). Captures
# are the new frames: the picture frame (right upper box) must show the greyed world, not black.
reject Script error
waitmenu
console thingamabob
newgame
waitline 15 Saved savea
waitfor 15 IsGreater(HP(GetLeader()),0)
console @BlockUnit(GetLeader(),0)
waitwindow 15 battle

# Escape menu, Save (rectangles from menus.reg): the new save's entry is selected.
key esc
waitwindow 10 escmenu
click 395 120
waitwindow 10 saveload
move 400 20
frames 5
freeze
capture save
unfreeze
# OK (region 0) saves into a new folder and closes the windows.
clickregion 0
waitline 15 Saved save1
waitwindow 10 battle

# Escape menu, Load: the first save is selected and its picture shown; then the second.
key esc
waitwindow 10 escmenu
click 395 187
waitwindow 10 saveload
move 400 20
frames 5
freeze
capture load
unfreeze
clickregion 4
frames 10
freeze
capture load2
unfreeze
# Cancel (region 1), then Esc closes the menu.
clickregion 1
frames 10
key esc
frames 10
check IsAlive(GetLeader())
report
quit
